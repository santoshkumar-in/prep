## Q3 — PDF Normalisation and Moments

$$\int_0^1 kx(1-x)\,dx = k\int_0^1(x-x^2)\,dx = k\left[\frac{x^2}{2}-\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^1 = k\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{3}\right) = k\cdot\frac{1}{6} = 1$$

$$\boxed{k = 6}$$

**Find $E[X]$:**
$$E[X] = \int_0^1 x\cdot6x(1-x)\,dx = 6\int_0^1(x^2-x^3)\,dx = 6\left[\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{4}\right] = 6\cdot\frac{1}{12} = \boxed{\frac{1}{2}}$$
