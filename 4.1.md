## What to Optimise — Complete Guide with Practice

---

### The Core Framework First

Every ML problem reduces to:

$$\text{Choose the RIGHT metric based on what MISTAKE is more costly}$$

| Mistake type | Name | Formula | Cost |
|-------------|------|---------|------|
| Predict positive when actually negative | False Positive (FP) | — | Varies |
| Predict negative when actually positive | False Negative (FN) | — | Varies |

---

### The Decision Table

| Situation | Most Costly Mistake | Optimise | Avoid |
|-----------|-------------------|---------|-------|
| Cancer detection | Missing cancer (FN) | **Recall** | FN |
| Spam filter | Blocking legit email (FP) | **Precision** | FP |
| Drug side effects | Missing side effect (FN) | **Recall** | FN |
| Search engine results | Showing irrelevant results (FP) | **Precision** | FP |
| Fraud detection | Missing fraud (FN) | **Recall** | FN |
| Criminal sentencing | Jailing innocent (FP) | **Precision** | FP |
| Balanced concern | Both FP and FN matter equally | **F1 score** | Both |
| Imbalanced dataset | Can't trust accuracy | **F1 or AUC** | Accuracy |
